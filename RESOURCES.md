# LLM Safety Classification Resources

## Knowledge

- [Paper: WildGuard: Open One-stop Moderation Tools for Safety Risks, Jailbreaks, and Refusals of LLMs (Lin et al., 2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.18495)
  Primary benchmark and dataset source. Explains how WildGuardMix was constructed, synthetic adversarial prompts, and human annotation audits.
- [Paper: BERT: Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional Transformers for Language Understanding (Devlin et al., 2018)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.04805)
  Foundational transformer architecture paper. Use for: understanding bidirectional self-attention, masked language modeling, and fine-tuning on classification tasks.
- [Paper: GloVe: Global Vectors for Word Representation (Pennington et al., EMNLP 2014)](https://nlp.stanford.edu/pubs/glove.pdf)
  Core pre-trained word embedding methodology. Use for: understanding log-bilinear matrix factorization over word co-occurrence stats.
- [Guide: ACL 2023 / 2025 Paper Author Guidelines & Style](https://2023.aclweb.org/)
  Official formatting and structural standard for the 7–8 page research report.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [Hugging Face Community & Forums](https://discuss.huggingface.co/)
  Use for: transformer fine-tuning best practices, memory management on GPUs, and tokenizer edge cases.
- [r/MachineLearning & r/LanguageTechnology](https://reddit.com/r/LanguageTechnology)
  Use for: high-signal discussions on safety guardrails, prompt injection defenses, and red-teaming benchmarks.
