# Mission: LLM Safety Text Classification & Moderation

## Why
Master the end-to-end machine learning and deep learning pipeline built for the CSE440 NLP project (Multi-Class Classification of Harmful and Adversarial Prompts). This knowledge will enable meaningful contributions to the final research report (ACL format), presentation recording, viva defense, and bonus extension tasks.

## Success looks like
- Clearly explain the business problem, dataset properties, and the 4-class taxonomy from memory.
- Articulate the data flow and transformation steps from raw text to TF-IDF, GloVe embeddings, and BERT subword tokens.
- Understand the architectural intuition, trade-offs, and failure modes across all 10 models (3 Traditional ML, 6 RNN variants, 1 Transformer).
- Confidently answer questions during the project viva regarding modeling decisions, evaluation metrics (Macro F1 vs. Accuracy), and generalization gaps.
- Lead or contribute to writing key sections of the 7–8 page ACL report and implementing bonus improvements (e.g., model ensembling, web app deployment).

## Constraints
- Start with high-level intuition, conceptual diagrams, and holistic data flow first.
- Progressively dive into deeper mathematical and code aspects on subsequent prompts.
- Maintain alignment with the CSE440 project guidelines and deliverables.

## Out of scope
- Theoretical proofs unnecessary for explaining and analyzing the classification pipeline.
- Training completely unrelated generative LLMs (focus remains on multi-class discriminative safety classification).
